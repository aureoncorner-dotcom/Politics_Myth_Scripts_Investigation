## Meta upgrade · 6 September 2026

**The strongest shared addition: a myth can preserve a political arrangement and become a mold for making the next one.** The research question then has two parts: what might have produced the story, and what people subsequently do with it. Each part needs its own evidence.

This packet drafts conceptual and methodological additions to the two projects. It contains:

- [Fossilized Monkey Politics — proposed essay additions](FOSSILIZED_MONKEY_POLITICS_META_ADDITIONS.md): the fossil/mold distinction, survival versus agency, a more precise capture test, and targeted revisions to existing passages.
- [Monkey Myth Matrix — proposed v0.22 method](MONKEY_MYTH_MATRIX_v0.22_META_PROPOSAL.md): linked claim, change, and source records that put the existing categories to work together.
- [Source and branch record](SOURCE_RECORD.md): which local versions were inspected, their differences, and the limits of this review.

The Matrix already separates motif evidence from interpretation, includes counterexamples, and has voice, exit, promotion, and adaptation fields. The Olympus branch already adds representation, route, history, closure, consequence, and relation typing. This upgrade connects those parts and makes the inferential steps easier to inspect.

Three additions do the most work:

1. **Origin, transmission, and use get separate claims.** Evidence that a later institution uses a story does not by itself recover the story's original cause.
2. **Survival gets a description.** A name, a ritual role, a right to speak, and a power to decide can survive or disappear separately. “The remainder survives” becomes a question with several possible answers.
3. **Support gets a family tree.** Three rows or translations drawing on one passage remain related observations. An edition correction can then reach every dependent reading.
