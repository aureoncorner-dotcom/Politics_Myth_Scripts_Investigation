## 1. Anti-retrofit controls

1. A fact that occurred on or before 17 August does not count as a prospective hit merely because it was discovered or compiled later.

5. A route is not a consequence. Chronology, proximity, shared vendors, common ideology, repeated names, and symbolic fit do not establish intent, coordination, common command, or causation.
6. Palace Drift remains NOT ESTABLISHED unless independent evidence supports material pre-review influence, resistance to disclosure/challenge/replacement, and at least one additional Palace criterion.

## 2. Geometry companion — method only, zero evidentiary weight

The GQG Core Card v0.3 and Hidden Quotient v1.1 sharpen the audit grammar but do not add evidence to any political or economic claim.

**No silent witness change.** A change in source, context, witness, observation, normalization, projection, claim rule, or realization must be declared where it acts. Transport of a conclusion across a changed architecture requires an explicit preservation/descent condition.

**Non-substitution / conjunctive promotion.** Separate witness architectures may support C1 AND C2 without licensing C1 → C2 or a stronger C unless a declared inference rule or source-bearing bridge supports the promotion. Multiple compatible corridor findings therefore remain multiple findings until an actual join is recovered.

**Provenance is not truth.** A frozen digest, timestamp, execution record, or archival lineage can establish identity and chronology of an artifact; it does not by itself establish semantic correctness, evidentiary independence, causation, or physical truth.

These rules were added after the 17 August freeze and therefore may constrain future scoring, but they may not be credited as part of the original prediction.

## 11. GQG v0.8 O-B-E prospective patch — effective 31 August 2026

**Status:** append-only measurement upgrade. This section does not rewrite the 17 August frozen baseline, reclassify prior rows as prospectively coded, or add external-world evidence. The separate retrospective calibration companion records why the earlier politics rows cannot supply a strict constraint-demotion rate.

### 11.1 Governing event state

For politics / Coup / Corridors events first logged after this patch, retain the claim-relative event state:

`X = (O, B, E)`

- `O` = the supplied object, institution, rule, jurisdiction, process, or task remains materially observable.
- `B` = the specifically named constraint retains governing force over the eligible transition.
- `E` = an added or existing route can materially alter adjudication, mediation, bypass, refusal, review, or exit.

A strict constraint-demotion event is:

`D+ := O=YES, B_before=YES, B_after=NO, E_before=NO, E_after=YES`

Candidate route introduction without demonstrated binding loss is:

`A+ := O=YES, E increases, B=UNKNOWN or unresolved`

Matched preserved control is:

`D- := O=YES, B=YES, E=NO`

Events with `O=NO` or full displacement belong to a separate destruction / substitution family.

### 11.2 Pre-event freeze

Before an event is scored, record whenever the evidence permits:

- event / cluster ID;
- frozen Coup move or corridor object;
- the exact object `O` being tracked;
- the exact constraint whose governing status is at issue;
- `B_before = YES / NO / PARTIAL / UNKNOWN`;
- the route whose activation or absence is at issue;
- `E_before = YES / NO / PARTIAL / UNKNOWN`;
- source or quotation supporting the pre-state;
- known alternate routes, courts, appeals, state resistance, review mechanisms, or other counter-architecture already active.

If `B_before` is not established as `YES`, the row cannot demonstrate demotion. If `E_before` is not established as `NO`, the row cannot demonstrate activation from zero.

### 11.3 Post-event coding

After the event, record separately:

- `O_after` / object preservation;
- `B_after = YES / NO / PARTIAL / UNKNOWN`;
- `E_after = YES / NO / PARTIAL / UNKNOWN`;
- exact support for every `NO` or `PARTIAL` coordinate;
- whether a court, state, appeal, review body, statutory route, or other counter-architecture changed the result;
- deletion test: if the added layer is removed while the substantive event is held fixed, does the object survive and does the original constraint-governed route return?;
- matched same-case control where available;
- final class: `D+ / A+ / D- / SEPARATE / UNKNOWN`.

A route is coded `YES` only when it can alter the architecture. Mere mention of an actor, reviewer, rule, option, or institution is not route activation.

### 11.4 Prospective row template

`Date | Event/cluster | Frozen object/move | O | Named constraint | B_before | B_after | Named route | E_before | E_after | Brake/control | Deletion test | Class | State effect / limit`

### 11.5 Counting rule

Do not report a strict `D+` rate from rows missing the pre-state. Retain numerator, denominator, `UNKNOWN` count, and event/cluster structure beside every reported rate.

The target quantity, once an adjudicable denominator exists, is:

`P(B_down AND E_up | O=1)`

against matched preserved controls with `O=1, B=1, E=0`.

Prior ledger rows remain valid at their original descriptive classifications. Retrospective O-B-E recoding may be used for calibration or acquisition planning only and must remain labeled retrospective.

### 11.6 Political interpretation guard

- movement is not automatically demotion;
- pressure is not automatically binding loss;
- an already-existing appeal or review channel is not a newly activated route;
- judicial or federalist repair is positive counter-architecture and must remain visible;
- route does not imply consequence, intent, motive, common command, or prophetic operation;
- GQG / Hidden Quotient constrains the scoring method and contributes zero political evidence.

**Patch checksum:** freeze the pre-state; name the constraint; name the route; preserve the brake; code `UNKNOWN` instead of inventing a coordinate; promote only when all required coordinates are actually present.
